### Cart URL

If the cart page in your shop is not `/cart` then you can tell Sledge by changing it in the cart url settings. The cart URL is used for redirection after the customer clicks add to cart on the wishlist page or instant search results

Using the [Custom Component - Product Card](/custom-components/global#product-card) will be disabled this setting.<br/>

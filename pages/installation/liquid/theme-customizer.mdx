# Start using Sledge and Liquid Theme Customizer

Prior using all Sledge's widgets, follow the steps below to start integrating Sledge into your Shopify theme.

## Quickstart

<Steps>
### Activate Sledge Core

- In the Shopify admin, click on `Apps` in the left-hand menu > search and choose `Sledge`.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_SEARCH_SLEDGE}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- In the Sledge dashboard, you can click `Theme Installation`.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_FIRST_STEP}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- Select theme then click `Activate`.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_GLOBAL_ACTIVATE}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- You will be redirected to the App embeds, then click `Save` button located in the upper-right corner.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto max-h-[459px] border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

### Implement widget

Once the Sledge app is installed and configured, we're going to try implement product filters widget.
On `Next Step` scroll to the `Product Filter` option, click `Integrate to my Theme`.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto max-h-[459px] border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_PLP}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- Then click `Add widget to my theme`.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto max-h-[459px] border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_PLP_WIDGET}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- You will be directed to the Editor, and the Product Filter will have been added automatically.

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto max-h-[459px] border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_PLP_ADDING}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- Click and Drag the `⋮⋮` icon up or down to move it to the position you want.
- You can adjust the maximum width of the `Product Filter`

<Image
  className="mt-3 rounded-[24px] w-auto max-h-[459px] border border-[rgba(67,198,172,1)]"
  src={IMAGE_LIQUID_SHOPIFY_APP_EMBED_PLP_CUSTOMIZE}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

- When ready, `Save` your changes.

### Widget completely running

Open your collections page and Sledge's product filters widget is now ready to be used.

<Image
  className="mt-3 w-auto max-h-[359px]"
  src={IMAGE_APPS_IS_PF_RESULT}
  alt="sledge-image-docs"
/>

</Steps>
